South America Market Entry & Demand Generation Program

To support expansion efforts in South America, I developed a complete market entry and lead generation framework designed to increase brand visibility and establish the company as a trusted industry expert. The project began with market research, audience analysis, persona development, and lead identification in a region where the company had limited direct market presence and no localized digital infrastructure. Based on these findings, I created a Spanish-language communication strategy centered around education rather than direct product promotion. The initiative included a dedicated landing page, a multi-stage email nurturing sequence, LinkedIn communication concepts, and a technical webinar series designed to provide practical value while introducing the company's expertise to a new audience. The project demonstrated how research-driven communication, localized content, and educational marketing can help build trust and create engagement in previously untapped markets. Skills: International Marketing · Market Research · Lead Generation · Content Strategy · Webinar Marketing · Project Management
